# AI usage disclosure

Generative AI was used in the making of MIReasy. This page says which tools were used, for what, and how the results were checked.

## Tools

- Claude Code (Anthropic), with the Claude Opus and the Claude Fable 5.1 and 5.2 models.

## Where and for what

- **Code:** writing and refactoring the code.
- **Tests and configuration:** writing the test scripts and the files for packaging and automated testing.
- **Documentation:** drafting and editing the documentation and the README.

## What the authors did

- The authors decided what MIReasy does. The features, the measures, the use cases and the design are theirs.
- The authors checked every line of the documentation.
- The authors tested the toolbox by hand on their own audio.

## How the results were checked

- Every change is run against an automated test suite of more than 300 tests, on Linux and macOS.
- The measures are tested on signals where the right answer is known.
- The licenses and the references were checked against their original sources.

The authors are responsible for everything in this project.
